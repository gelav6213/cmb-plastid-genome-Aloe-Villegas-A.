## Short plastome summary
- The chloroplast genome of *Aloe vera* (NCBI accession: NC_035506.1) is a complete circular genome consisting of 152,875 bp with a GC content of 37.66%. It exhibits the typical quadripartite structure of plant plastid genomes, comprising an 83,505-bp LSC region, a 16,178-bp SSC region, and two 26,596-bp IR regions. The genome contains 131 annotated genes, including 85 protein-coding genes, 38 tRNA genes, and 8 rRNA genes, which support photosynthesis, energy production, transcription, and protein synthesis.
