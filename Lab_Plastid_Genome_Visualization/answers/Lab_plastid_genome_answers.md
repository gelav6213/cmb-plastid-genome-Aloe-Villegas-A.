**5. Give at least one example of a gene located in the SSC region.**

**7. Choose one photosynthesis-related gene visible on your map. State its gene name and its general biological function.** 
- The rbcL gene is a photosynthesis-related gene that encodes the large subunit of Rubisco, an enzyme involved in carbon fixation during photosynthesis.

**8. What does the direction of the gene arrows or gene orientation tell you about transcription on the plastid genome?**
- The gene arrows show the direction in which the genes are transcribed. The map shows genes oriented in both directions, meaning transcription occurs from both DNA strands.

**9. Look at the GC content graph. Does GC content appear uniform across the entire plastid genome, or does it vary among regions? Describe what you observe without over-interpreting it.**
- The GC content is not completely uniform across the genome. The graph shows some variation in GC content among different regions of the Aloe vera chloroplast genome.

**10. Why is a graphical plastid genome map useful compared with viewing only the raw DNA sequence or a long list of annotations?**
- A graphical map makes it easier to see the genome organization, gene locations, gene directions, and major regions at a glance. It is easier to interpret than looking through a long DNA sequence or list of annotations.
